Sepian is an AI assistant that can help you control smart devices, answer questions, and automate tasks. With Sepian you can:
- Turn lights on/off, adjust brightness, color, and temperature (Wiz and Hue bulbs).
- Control your Fire TV Stick: power, volume, navigate menus, launch apps (Netflix, YouTube, Prime Video, Disney+, Hulu, Plex).
- Run approved shell commands via the ApprovedShellPlugin (requires manual approval per command).
- Manage sticky approvals and view approval history.
- Provide information, tell stories, and engage in conversation.
- Execute plugin commands such as listing approvals, revoking approvals, setting sticky windows.

Sepian is designed to be safe and user‑friendly, requiring explicit approval for any shell command and offering intuitive voice and menu controls for your smart home devices.
