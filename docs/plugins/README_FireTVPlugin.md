# FireTVPlugin
The FireTVPlugin provides functionality for controlling a Fire TV device within the Sepian workspace.

### Commands
* `home`: Goes home on the Fire TV device.
* `power`: Powers off the Fire TV device.
* `up`: Navigates up on the Fire TV device.
* `down`: Navigates down on the Fire TV device.
* `left`: Navigates left on the Fire TV device.
* `right`: Navigates right on the Fire TV device.

### Usage
To use the FireTVPlugin, simply call the desired command. For example:
* To go home on the Fire TV device, call `home`.
